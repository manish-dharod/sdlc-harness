cannot move from `Backlog` to `Open` while any question with `Blocks: tasks` is
unanswered. `planner (Phase: intake)` opens questions; the owner closes them.

## Status legend

- `Open` — waiting on the owner
- `Answered` — owner provided a decision; planner (Phase: intake) folds it back into SPEC.md
- `Withdrawn` — turned out to be a non-issue; explain why
